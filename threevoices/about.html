About {{ site.threevoices.title }}

{{ site.threevoices.title }} records how conversational AI is applied in autism care, agriculture and space technologies: one use case from each, every month, with a step‑by‑step account of how it works.

In scope

Chatbots, voice assistants and language‑model tools that people talk or type to, used in autism care (Medical · Autism), agriculture, or space technologies.

What counts as a source

News outlets, peer‑reviewed journals, universities, space agencies and other public bodies, research institutes and non‑profits.

What is left out

Vendor case studies, AI‑company blog posts, consulting reports and social‑media testimonials, even when they tell a good story.

The fine print

  • Each issue picks one use case per domain: the best-sourced or most recent that month.
  • Many projects are pilots. Where results are early, self‑reported or from one small study, the case says so in a “Read with care” note.
  • Health tools here, for autism or astronaut care, are research or pilot systems, not replacements for clinicians.
